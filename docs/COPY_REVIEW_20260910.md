Method: coreyhaines31/marketingskills, product-marketing → copywriting → copy-editing. Owner confirmed Indonesian Gen Z and natural mixed English/Indonesian, not formal full-Indonesian copy. Scope is interface copy only, not curator-authored content, uploaded banners or legal policies.

## Applied direction and reasons
- Hero: retain OUTFIT CHECK, DONE RIGHT.; supporting line **Good outfits. Less overthinking.** identifies the outfit-indecision benefit. Body: **Dari ngantor sampai weekend plans, temukan look yang kamu banget. Cek produknya, lalu buka link ke toko.** CTA remains **Explore looks**. Clarifies external shopping.
- Discovery: keep Match Your Vibe and Tailored for You; describe style selection and preference-based suggestions without promising perfect matches or AI.
- Trending: **Community Favorites.** with an explicit likes description; do not mislabel likes as saves.
- Products: **Easy to Mix. Ready for Repeat.** plus everyday basics/statement pieces; remove unsubstantiated 'terbukti'.
- Journal: describe color, proportions and mix & match ideas instead of a second abstract slogan. CTA remains Baca semua Journal.
- Curators: **Different styles, real points of view.** gives creator discovery its own benefit, distinct from product discovery.
- Account: **Welcome back.** / **Your style starts here.** with concrete saving, following and request benefits; existing sign-in actions unchanged.
- Studio: **Let’s build your look.** / **Refine your look.**; steps explain photos, 2–5 products, preview and public publication. Labels use Judul look, Harga saat kurasi (Rp), Link produk utama. Toggle uses Tutup detail to describe behavior.
- Request: plans/budget/vibe language; success message points to the profile, not the internal admin queue. No response-time promises or changed budget rules.

## Alternatives, not implemented
Hero supporting line: **Your next look starts here.** (discovery emphasis) or **Less scrolling. More styling.** (more editorial; avoid presenting as a measured time saving).
Hero CTA: **Find your next look** (personal invitation) or current **Explore looks** (short and clear; selected).
Studio headline: **Your next outfit edit.** (editorial) versus selected **Let’s build your look.** (more action-oriented).

## Seven editing passes
Clarity: external shop destination, preview/publication distinction and price context explicit.
Voice: conversational kamu/kami, familiar English fashion terms, no forced viral slang.
Benefits: discovery, product details and saved favorites over technical mechanisms.
Proof: removed 'terbukti'; no fabricated users, testimonials, statistics, conversion lifts or guarantees.
Specificity: concrete occasion examples and existing 2–5 product constraint.
Emotion: relief from outfit indecision, without body shame or purchase pressure.
Risk: account actions, local-draft limitations and publication boundaries preserved; no legal/privacy-policy promises added.
